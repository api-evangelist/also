This is how I start almost every day—taking my kid to school, saying 'hi' to friends and neighbors along the way. It’s one of my favorite moments because it’s one of the few times when I can be fully present—not focused on a screen or a to-do list or anything other than the road in front of me, the wind in my face and the time the two of us have together. It sounds simple because it is.
