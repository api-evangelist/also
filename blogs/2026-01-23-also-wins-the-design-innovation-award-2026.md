The TM-B is officially an award-winning ebike. And not just any award, either, but one of the toughest, most prestigious accolades in the bike and outdoor industries, the Design & Innovation Award. Unlike some industry awards, which are won through press releases, pitch decks, and tricked-out brand campaigns, the only way to bring home a Design & Innovation Award is the hard way: through months of rigorous, real-world testing by an independent jury.
